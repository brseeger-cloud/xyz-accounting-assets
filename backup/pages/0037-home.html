How it works

One portal, with a human at the end of it.

  1. Create your portal account

    Tap Start my return, or Client Portal at the top of any page. Use your email, Google or a one-tap magic link. Nothing is charged until your return is lodged.

  2. Verify your identity, sign one pack

    A three-minute electronic ID check on your phone, then one document: our engagement, your tax agent authority and TFN consent. Why every client is verified.

  3. Complete your intake form

    Plain questions about income, work expenses, home office, vehicle, property and trades. Stop and come back; your answers are saved.

  4. Upload your documents

    Payment summaries, receipts and rental statements go into Files inside the portal, never an email thread. Ask us anything in Messages.

  5. We prepare, you approve

    A registered agent prepares the return and flags anything missing. You sign the lodgement declaration in the portal and we lodge.

  6. Settle the fee

    Pay your invoice in Billing, or sign the fee-from-refund authority and we take the fee from your refund instead (an extra $44).
