The calculators

Are the calculators really free?
Yes. No email, no account, no limit on how many times you use them. We charge for preparing and lodging a return, not for arithmetic.

How accurate are they?
The tax engine is unit tested against the ATO published tables at every bracket boundary, and for every $1,000 step from $0 to $500,000 across three financial years. Where a figure is provisional because the ATO has not yet indexed it, the calculator says so. They are still estimates: a real assessment also uses the data the ATO holds about you.

Which financial year should I pick?
The year the income was earned. A return being lodged now covers the year ended 30 June 2026, which is 2025-26. Pick 2026-27 if you are working out take-home pay for the year you are in.

Can I share a calculation with someone?
Yes. Every calculator writes your figures into the address bar, so copying the URL shares exactly what you were looking at. Nothing identifying is included.

Lodging with us

What does it cost?
Individual returns are $99, investments $179, sole traders $299. Fixed, quoted before we start, and deductible next year. Full pricing is published on the pricing page.

How long does it take?
The intake form takes about ten minutes. We usually have a draft return back to you within two business days, and the ATO typically assesses an electronically lodged return within two weeks.

Can the fee come out of my refund?
Yes, for individual returns, and it costs $44. The refund goes to our trust account, we deduct the fee and forward the balance, usually within a business day. Pay the fee up front instead and the $44 does not apply.

Do you handle late or unlodged returns?
Regularly. If you have several years outstanding we can put them on a lodgement program and work through them in order. The usual outcome is money back rather than a bill, and the ATO treats voluntary catch-up very differently from being chased.

Privacy and security

Do you ask for my tax file number?
Never on this website, including the intake form. TFNs are subject to the Privacy (Tax File Number) Rule 2015. We collect yours through a secure channel once an accountant is working on your return.

What do the calculators send to your servers?
Nothing. Every calculation runs in your browser. We use privacy-focused analytics that records that a calculator was used, never the figures you typed.

Why do I have to verify my identity for a simple tax return?
Because a tax return is exactly what identity thieves lodge. Every client — every return, every service — completes a three-minute electronic ID check on their phone before we start: a licence or passport, a selfie, and a match against government records. It is included in the fee, the TPB requires tax agents to verify every client, and it keeps your refund out of someone else’s account. How the check works.

Are you actually registered?
Yes — we are a Registered Tax Agent. Anyone charging a fee to prepare or lodge a return must be registered with the Tax Practitioners Board, and the register is free to search. Check us, and check anyone else you are considering.

Ready to lodge it?

A registered agent prepares it, checks it, and lodges it.

Most refunds land within two weeks of assessment. The fee is fixed and quoted before we start — nothing is charged until you approve the finished return.
