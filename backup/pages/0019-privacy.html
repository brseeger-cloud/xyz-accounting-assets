Privacy policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

XYZ Accounting is a trading name of BRS Advisory Pty Ltd (ABN 83 666 883 398), which is bound by the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, in relation to tax file numbers, by the Privacy (Tax File Number) Rule 2015.

Tax file numbers

We do not collect tax file numbers through this website. There is no TFN field in any calculator, in the contact form, or in the lodgement intake form.

Where we need your TFN to prepare and lodge a return, we collect it after the engagement begins, through a secure channel our accountants will direct you to. We use it only for the purpose you gave it to us — dealing with the ATO on your behalf — and we do not disclose it to anyone other than the ATO except where the law requires it. Please do not send a TFN by email.

What the calculators collect

Nothing. Every calculation on this site runs entirely in your browser. The figures you type are never transmitted to us and are never stored on our servers.

Calculator figures do appear in the address bar so that a result can be shared or bookmarked. If you go on to lodge, you enter your figures in the intake form yourself.

The forms on this site

The contact form and the lodgement intake form are both processed by Jotform, which stores what you submit on its servers under its own privacy policy, and delivers it to us. Neither form asks for a tax file number. If you would rather not use Jotform, you can email or phone us instead — the details are on the contact page.

What we collect when you engage us

  • Identity and contact details: name, date of birth, address, email, phone
  • Income and deduction information, and the documents supporting it
  • Bank account details, for refunds
  • Your TFN, collected securely as described above
  • Records of our correspondence with you and with the ATO on your behalf

How we use it

To prepare and lodge your return, to deal with the ATO on your behalf, to meet our obligations as a registered tax agent, and to contact you about your engagement. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your tax information for marketing.

Who we disclose it to

  • The Australian Taxation Office, as your appointed agent
  • Jotform, which processes the enquiry and intake forms on this site
  • Our practice management, document storage and e-signature providers, under contract
  • Anyone you specifically authorise, such as a mortgage broker requesting a copy of a return
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are legally required to

Where a service provider stores information outside Australia, we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Analytics and cookies

We use privacy-focused analytics that does not use cookies, does not track you across websites and does not collect personal information. It records that a page was viewed and that a calculator was used. It never records the figures you entered.

How long we keep it

Tax records are kept for at least five years from the date a return is lodged, as required by the Taxation Administration Act 1953 and our professional obligations. After that we destroy or de-identify information we no longer need.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing enquiries@xyzaccounting.com.au. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first at enquiries@xyzaccounting.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
