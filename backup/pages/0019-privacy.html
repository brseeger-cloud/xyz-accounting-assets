Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

XYZ Accounting is a trading name of BRS Advisory Pty Ltd (ABN 83 666 883 398), which is bound by the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, in relation to tax file numbers, by the Privacy (Tax File Number) Rule 2015.

Tax file numbers

We do not collect tax file numbers through this website. There is no TFN field in any calculator or in any form on this site.

Where we need your TFN to prepare and lodge a return, we collect it after the engagement begins, through a secure channel our accountants will direct you to. We use it only for the purpose you gave it to us — dealing with the ATO on your behalf — and we do not disclose it to anyone other than the ATO except where the law requires it. Please do not send a TFN by email.

What the calculators collect

Nothing. Every calculation on this site runs entirely in your browser. The figures you type are never transmitted to us and are never stored on our servers.

Calculator figures do appear in the address bar so that a result can be shared or bookmarked. If you go on to lodge, you enter your figures in our client portal yourself.

The forms on this site and the client portal

The contact, enquiry and quote forms on this site are processed by Jotform, which stores what you submit on its servers under its own privacy policy, and delivers it to us. None of these forms asks for a tax file number.

When you start a return or engage us, you sign up to our secure client portal, where you give us your details, upload documents, sign engagement documents and approve your return. The portal is operated for us by a third-party portal provider under contract. If you would rather not use the forms or the portal to make an enquiry, you can email or phone us instead — the details are on the contact page.

Identity verification

We verify the identity of every client before we provide any service, whether that is a tax return, a registration or advice. This meets our obligations under the Tax Practitioners Board’s proof-of-identity requirements for tax agents and, for designated services such as company and trust formation, under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 (Cth). Where the client is a company, trust, partnership or fund, we verify each director, trustee, partner and beneficial owner.

The check is carried out through an Australian AML/CTF compliance platform using accredited electronic identity verification technology. To complete it you will be asked to photograph a government-issued identity document (such as a driver licence or passport) and take a short selfie. The platform collects:

  • Images of your identity document and the details on it: name, date of birth, address, document number, expiry
  • Your facial image and biometric data derived from it, used to confirm the document belongs to you (liveness and face-match)
  • The result of a check of the document against the issuing authority’s records through the Australian Government’s Document Verification Service
  • The result of screening your name against sanctions and politically-exposed-person lists
  • Technical data about the device and session used to complete the check

Document images and biometric data are collected and stored by our identity verification provider on our behalf and are not stored on this website or sent to us by email. We receive the verification outcome and the verified details, which are recorded on your client file. Biometric information is sensitive information under the Privacy Act; it is collected only with your consent, used only to verify your identity, and is not used for any other purpose or disclosed to anyone except regulators where the law requires it. If you do not consent to the electronic check, we can accept certified copies of your documents instead; if identity cannot be established either way, we are unable to act for you.

What we collect when you engage us

  • Identity and contact details: name, date of birth, address, email, phone, and the identity verification records described above
  • Income and deduction information, and the documents supporting it
  • Bank account details, for refunds
  • Your TFN, collected securely as described above
  • Records of our correspondence with you and with the ATO on your behalf

How we use it

To prepare and lodge your return, to deal with the ATO on your behalf, to meet our obligations as a registered tax agent and as a reporting entity under the AML/CTF Act, to verify your identity, and to contact you about your engagement. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your tax information for marketing.

Who we disclose it to

  • The Australian Taxation Office, as your appointed agent
  • Jotform, which processes the contact, enquiry and quote forms on this site
  • Our client portal provider, which hosts the portal where you give us your details, upload documents and approve work
  • Our identity verification provider, which carries out identity verification and AML/CTF screening on our behalf
  • AUSTRAC, where the AML/CTF Act requires us to report
  • Our practice management, document storage and e-signature providers, under contract
  • Anyone you specifically authorise, such as a mortgage broker requesting a copy of a return
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where we are legally required to

Some of our service providers store information outside Australia. In particular, our client portal provider stores portal data in the United States. Where a service provider stores information outside Australia, we take reasonable steps to ensure they handle it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Analytics and cookies

We use privacy-focused analytics that does not use cookies, does not track you across websites and does not collect personal information. It records that a page was viewed and that a calculator was used. It never records the figures you entered.

How long we keep it

Tax records are kept for at least five years from the date a return is lodged, as required by the Taxation Administration Act 1953 and our professional obligations. Identity verification records are kept for seven years after our relationship with you ends, as required by the AML/CTF Act. After that we destroy or de-identify information we no longer need.

Accessing and correcting your information

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it, by emailing enquiries@xyzaccounting.com.au. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for a reasonable request.

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first at enquiries@xyzaccounting.com.au. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au or on 1300 363 992.
