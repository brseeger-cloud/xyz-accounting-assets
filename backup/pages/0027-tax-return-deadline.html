The full timeline

  1. 1 July 2026

    Lodgement opens

    You can lodge from 1 July, but employers, banks, health funds and share registries have until late July to report. Lodging in the first fortnight usually means amending later.

  2. Late July 2026

    Pre-fill data lands

    Once your income statement is marked "tax ready" and the third-party data is in, a return can be prepared against what the ATO already holds rather than from memory.

  3. 31 October 2026

    Self-lodgement deadline

    The hard deadline if you are lodging your own return. It is also the date by which you must be on a registered agent's client list to get the extension.

  4. 15 May 2027

    Registered agent deadline

    If you engaged a registered tax agent before 31 October, most individual returns are not due until 15 May the following year — roughly six and a half extra months.

Why the agent extension exists

It is not a loophole. The ATO gives registered agents a lodgement program because spreading millions of returns across the year is the only way the system copes. The extension belongs to the agent’s program, not to you personally, which is why the 31 October registration date is strict: after that, you are not on the program for that year.

A practical consequence people miss: the extension applies to lodgement. If you end up owing tax, the payment date is set when the return is assessed, so lodging in May does not mean paying in May — but it does not defer a debt you already have.

If you have already missed it

Lodge anyway, and lodge soon. Penalties accrue by the 28-day period, so the difference between acting this week and next month is measurable. The ATO’s stated position is that it does not generally apply penalties to a first late return where a refund is due — and most late individual returns are refunds.

If several years are outstanding, an agent can put the whole set on a lodgement program and work through them in order. We do this regularly; it is far less confronting than people expect, and the usual outcome is money back rather than a bill.

Other dates worth having

  • 28 October, 28 February, 28 April, 28 July — quarterly BAS due dates, extended by four weeks when lodged through an agent.
  • 28 July — super guarantee for the June quarter must be received by the fund, not just paid.
  • 30 June — the last day for a deductible expense, a super contribution to count towards the year’s cap, or a contract date to fall in this year for CGT.
  • 1 June — study loan indexation is applied to the outstanding balance.

Questions people actually ask

When is the tax return deadline for 2026?
For the year ended 30 June 2026, self-lodged returns are due by 31 October 2026. If you are on a registered tax agent's client list before that date, the deadline generally extends to 15 May 2027.
What is the tax return deadline with a tax agent?
Usually 15 May of the following year, and sometimes 5 June where the return will not result in a liability. The critical condition is that you must be registered with the agent before 31 October — engaging one in December does not retrospectively buy you the extension.
What happens if I lodge late?
The failure to lodge on time penalty is one penalty unit for each 28 days late, up to five units — currently $330 per unit for individuals, so up to $1,650. Interest also accrues on unpaid tax. The ATO frequently remits penalties for a first offence if you engage with them.
What if I have not lodged for several years?
Lodge them. The ATO treats voluntary disclosure very differently from being caught, and a registered agent can request a lodgement program that brings multiple years up to date without penalties in most cases. Many people who avoid it are actually owed refunds.
Do I even need to lodge a return?
If you had any tax withheld, yes — and you should, because you probably get it back. You also need to lodge if you earned above the tax-free threshold, ran a business, had a capital gain, or the ATO has asked you to. If none of that applies, you may only need a non-lodgement advice.
