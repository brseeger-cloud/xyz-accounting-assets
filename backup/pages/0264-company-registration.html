Company registration

Registering a company in Australia means applying to ASIC for an ACN. We handle the application, the share structure and the director IDs, and you get the certificate of registration, constitution and company register.

Government charges

Payable on top

ASIC charges a company registration fee, paid to the government on top of ours. We tell you the current amount before you commit.

How it runs

  1. 01 Tell us the proposed name, the directors and how you want shares split.
  2. 02 We check the name is available and confirm the structure makes sense for what you are actually doing.
  3. 03 We lodge with ASIC and register the company for its TFN and ABN.
  4. 04 You get the certificate, constitution and register, plus what to do next.
