    What makes an invoice a valid tax invoice

    If you are registered for GST and the sale is over $82.50 including GST, your customer needs a valid tax invoice before they can claim the GST credit. Get it wrong and they cannot claim — which is usually when they ring you to reissue it.

    Every tax invoice under $1,000 must show

    • That the document is intended to be a tax invoice
    • Your identity as the seller — business or trading name
    • Your ABN
    • The date the invoice was issued
    • A description of the items sold, including quantity where relevant
    • The GST amount, or a statement that the total price includes GST

    Once the total reaches $1,000 or more

    Add the buyer’s identity or their ABN. The generator above prompts you for it as soon as the total crosses that line.

    If you are not registered for GST

    You must not issue a tax invoice or charge GST. Tick the GST box off above and the document reissues as a plain invoice with no GST line — which is what you should be sending.

    Keep them for five years

    The ATO expects records supporting your return to be kept for five years from lodgement. A folder of PDFs is fine; a shoebox of thermal receipts that have faded to blank is not.

    Official source: ATO: Tax invoices.
