What the fee actually buys

A registered agent does three things software cannot. We reconcile your return against the data the ATO already holds, so what you lodge and what they expect agree. We apply judgement to the grey areas — apportionment, what counts as a repair, whether a course is deductible. And we sign it, which means the practice carries professional indemnity for the position taken.

If your return is one job, no investments and no deductions worth arguing about, myTax is free and perfectly adequate. We would rather say that than take $99 for typing in numbers the ATO already had.

The fee is deductible

The cost of managing your tax affairs is deductible in the year you pay it. At the 30% bracket a $99 fee costs you about $67 after tax — and the deduction is claimed on the return you lodge next year, including the one that claimed it.

Tax agent obligations

We are registered with the Tax Practitioners Board, number 26233684. That registration carries obligations to you under the Code of Professional Conduct — competence, independence, confidentiality, and telling you about anything that would materially affect your decision to engage us. Our tax agent obligations page sets out what that means and how to complain if we fall short.

Questions people actually ask

Is the fee deductible?
Yes. The cost of managing your tax affairs is deductible in the year you pay it, so this year's fee reduces next year's tax. At the 30% bracket a $99 fee costs you about $67 after tax.

Can the fee come out of my refund?
Yes, for individual returns. The ATO pays the refund to our trust account, we deduct the fee and forward the balance to you, usually within one business day of the refund landing.

What if my return turns out to be more complicated?
We tell you before we do the work, not after. If what you send us needs a higher tier or an add-on, you get a revised quote and you decide. We do not invoice a surprise.

Do you charge for the calculators?
No, and we never will. They are free, ungated and unlimited. We charge for preparing and lodging a return, which is the part that needs a registered agent.

What if I owe money instead of getting a refund?
The fee is the same — the work is the same. If you are facing a bill, an agent is usually worth more rather than less, because the deductions we find come straight off it.

Ready to lodge it?

A registered agent prepares it, checks it, and lodges it.

Most refunds land within two weeks of assessment. The fee is fixed and quoted before we start — nothing is charged until you approve the finished return.
