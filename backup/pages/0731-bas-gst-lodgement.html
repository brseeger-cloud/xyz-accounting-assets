Services

BAS & GST lodgement

Your business activity statement prepared from your books, checked by a registered agent and lodged on time — GST, PAYG withholding and PAYG instalments, without the quarterly scramble.

Our fee

$99 a quarter, inc. GST

Fixed per quarterly BAS from clean books, and included in every bookkeeping tier. Monthly BAS and catch-up quarters are quoted.

Government charges

None

Lodging a BAS is free. What you owe the ATO on the BAS itself is paid to the ATO, not to us.

Turnaround

Before the due date

Lodged through our agent portal, which can give extra time on quarterly BAS lodged online.

What’s included

  • GST reconciled against your bookkeeping and bank feeds, not just copied out of the software
  • PAYG withholding (W1 and W2) checked against payroll
  • PAYG instalments, including whether a varied instalment would suit you
  • Fuel tax credits where you are entitled to them
  • The figure you owe and its due date, sent to you before lodgement

How it runs

  1. 01 Verify your identity online — about three minutes on your phone, included in the fee. Every client, every service, no exceptions. How it works.
  2. 02 Give us access to your Xero, MYOB or QuickBooks file, or upload your records to the portal.
  3. 03 We reconcile the quarter and flag anything that does not look right.
  4. 04 You approve the BAS in the portal.
  5. 05 We lodge it under our agent number and send you the payment details.

Monthly, quarterly or annual?

Most small businesses lodge quarterly. Turnover of $20 million or more means monthly. How BAS lodgement frequency works. If your books are not up to date, bookkeeping covers the BAS as part of the monthly fee.
