One engine behind all of them

The brackets, the Medicare levy with its low-income shading, the marginal study loan scale, the low income tax offset and the s 25-130 standard deduction are implemented once, in versioned rate files that each cite their ATO source and the date they were checked. Every calculator on this page calls the same functions.

That matters more than it sounds. Most tax calculators on the web reimplement the scale per page, which is why the same income can produce three different answers on the same site. Ours cannot: the arithmetic runs in one place and is tested at every bracket boundary.

Want the underlying figures rather than a calculator? The tax rates page has the full bracket tables for all three years, and the guides explain what you can actually claim.
