How to lodge your tax return

You can lodge through myTax in your myGov account, through a registered tax agent, or on paper. Wait until late July so your income statement is marked tax ready and the ATO pre-fill data has landed.

Updated 21 August 2026 Current for 2025-26

Before you start

Wait until late July. Employers have until 14 July to finalise income statements, and banks, health funds, share registries and crypto exchanges report to the ATO through July. Lodging on 1 July means typing everything from memory and, quite often, amending later.

The signal to watch for is your income statement showing tax ready in myGov. Until then the figures are not final.

Have ready:

  • Your income statement, which appears in myGov automatically
  • Bank interest, dividend statements and any managed fund distributions
  • Private health insurance details, if you have cover
  • Receipts and records for anything you intend to claim
  • Your bank account details for the refund
  • Details of any capital gains: contract dates, purchase and sale costs

The three ways to lodge

myTax, through myGov. Free, and reasonable for a simple return: one job, some bank interest, a handful of deductions. Most of it is pre-filled and you confirm it. Due 31 October.

A registered tax agent. The agent prepares the return, reconciles it against the data the ATO holds, and lodges it. The fee is deductible next year. The lodgement deadline extends to 15 May the following year, provided you are on the agent’s client list before 31 October.

Paper. Still available, still slow — up to ten weeks to process. Order the forms from the ATO.

Whichever route you take, check the agent is registered on the Tax Practitioners Board public register. Only registered agents may charge a fee to prepare and lodge a return, and the register is free to search.

What happens after you lodge

The ATO issues a notice of assessment, usually within two weeks for an electronic lodgement. It either confirms the refund and pays it into your nominated account, or sets out what you owe and when.

If the assessment differs from what you expected, it is generally because ATO data disagreed with something you entered. The notice says which item.

If you make a mistake

Amend it. You have two years from the date of assessment for most individuals, and you can amend through myGov or through your agent. Voluntarily correcting an error is treated very differently from being found out — penalties are commonly reduced to nil for a genuine mistake disclosed before the ATO asks.

Do you even need to lodge?

If any tax was withheld from your pay, you almost certainly should, because you are probably owed some of it back. You must lodge if you earned above the tax-free threshold, ran a business, had a capital gain, or the ATO has asked you.

If none of that applies, you may only need to submit a non-lodgement advice, which tells the ATO not to expect a return and stops the reminders. Do I need to lodge a tax return? works through both cases in full.
