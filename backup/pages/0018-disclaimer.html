Calculator disclaimer

Last updated 21 August 2026

The calculators on this website provide estimates. They are not tax advice, they do not take your particular circumstances into account, and they do not create an accountant-client relationship. Your actual liability is determined by the Australian Taxation Office on assessment.

What the calculators model

  • The individual income tax scale for residents, foreign residents and working holiday makers
  • The Medicare levy, including the low-income reduction, and the Medicare levy surcharge
  • Compulsory study and training loan repayments
  • The low income tax offset and the seniors and pensioners tax offset
  • The ITAA 1997 s 25-130 standard deduction, from the 2026-27 income year
  • Capital gains for individuals, including the 50% discount and capital losses

What they do not model

  • The private health insurance rebate
  • Foreign income tax offsets and foreign residency part-years
  • A spouse’s unused seniors and pensioners offset
  • Family Tax Benefit, child support and other means-tested payments
  • Small business concessions, CGT rollovers and the main residence exemption
  • PAYG instalments already paid during the year
  • Prior year losses of any kind, other than capital losses you enter yourself

Where any of these apply to you, the estimate should be treated as a starting point rather than an answer.

Where the rates come from

Rates and thresholds are taken from ato.gov.au and stored in versioned configuration files, each recording the source and the date it was verified:

Rate verification dates
Financial year Verified Status
2026–27 2026-08-21 Partly provisional
2025–26 2026-08-21 Confirmed
2024–25 2026-08-21 Confirmed

“Partly provisional” means the ATO had not published an indexed threshold for that year at the verification date, so the prior year’s figure is carried forward. The affected rows are marked on the tax rates page and the calculators say so beside the result.

Rounding

Assessments are worked in whole dollars, with cents in taxable income discarded. The calculators do the same, then display figures rounded to the nearest dollar. Small differences against a payslip or a notice of assessment are expected and are not errors.
