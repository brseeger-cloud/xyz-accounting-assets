FAQs

Common questions

Is it really all online?

Yes. XYZ Accounting is online only. You sign up, upload documents, message us and sign everything in our client portal, so there are no appointments to book.

Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes — every client, for every service, before any work starts. It is a three-minute electronic check on your phone (licence or passport, a selfie, matched against government records) and it is included in the fee. For a company, trust or SMSF, each director, trustee or controlling owner does their own check. How it works.

Who prepares and lodges my work?

BRS Advisory Pty Ltd, a Registered Tax Agent. Every return and registration is prepared or reviewed by a qualified accountant before it is lodged.

How long does it take?

Each service shows its usual turnaround. Most registrations are done within a few business days. Returns depend on when we have your documents, and we confirm the timing with your quote.

What about government fees?

ASIC fees, the ATO SMSF levy and any stamp duty are separate from our fee. We show them before you start and pass them on at cost, without markup.

What happens after setup?

We can look after what comes next: BAS and GST lodgement, bookkeeping, the company’s annual ASIC review and the yearly tax return. Your registrations are yours, so you can move them to another accountant at any time.
