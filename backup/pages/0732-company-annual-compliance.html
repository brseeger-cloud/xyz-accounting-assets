Services

Company annual compliance

Everything ASIC expects of a Pty Ltd each year, handled for you — the annual review, the solvency resolution, registers and minutes — so the company stays in good standing and the directors stay out of trouble.

Our fee

$99 a year, inc. GST

Fixed per company per year. Changes during the year (new directors, share issues, address changes) are included.

Government charges

ASIC annual review fee

Set by ASIC each year and passed on at cost. A reduced fee applies to special purpose trustee companies.

Turnaround

Within the review window

Done inside the two months after your review date, before ASIC late fees apply.

What’s included

  • Checking the annual statement against the company’s real details on its review date
  • Paying the ASIC annual review fee on time
  • The directors’ solvency resolution, drafted and minuted within two months of the review date
  • Your registers of members, officers and share issues kept up to date
  • Minutes for the year’s key decisions, and ASIC change notices lodged within 28 days of a change

How it runs

  1. 01 Verify identity online — each director, trustee or controlling owner does a three-minute check on their phone, included in the fee. Every client, every service, no exceptions. How it works.
  2. 02 We take over as the company’s registered agent with ASIC, so the annual statement comes to us.
  3. 03 On the review date we check every detail ASIC holds and correct anything out of date.
  4. 04 We prepare the solvency resolution and minutes for the directors to sign in the portal.
  5. 05 We pay the review fee and file the paperwork in your company records.

Why this matters

ASIC charges late fees when the review fee or a change notice is late, and a company that stops paying can eventually be deregistered. Directors who allow a company to trade while insolvent can be personally liable — the annual solvency resolution is the moment to look at that properly.
