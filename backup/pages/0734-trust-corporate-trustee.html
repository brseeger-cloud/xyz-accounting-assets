Trust + corporate trustee

A discretionary family trust with a company as its trustee, set up together — so the trust’s assets sit apart from your personal ones and trustee changes later are a share transfer, not a re-papering of every asset.

Government charges

ASIC fee and any duty

The company registration fee and any stamp duty on the deed, which depends on your state. Passed on at cost.

Turnaround

3–5 business days

The trustee company is registered first, then the deed is prepared in its name and settled.

What’s in the bundle

  • A trustee company registered with ASIC, set up as a special purpose company where that suits, for the reduced annual fee
  • A discretionary trust deed naming the company as trustee
  • Settlement of the trust and guidance on stamping the deed in your state
  • ABN and TFN for the trust, and GST registration if it will trade
  • Advice on beneficiaries, the appointor and resolving distributions by 30 June

How it runs

  1. 01 Verify identity online — each director, trustee or controlling owner does a three-minute check on their phone, included in the fee. Every client, every service, no exceptions. How it works.
  2. 02 Tell us who will be directors, the appointor and the family members to benefit.
  3. 03 We register the trustee company.
  4. 04 We prepare the deed in the company’s name for you to sign and settle in the portal.
  5. 05 We register the trust’s ABN and TFN and send you the full record.
