Online Return

Individual tax return, lodged online by a registered agent

Send us your details online and an accountant prepares your return. We check every deduction your occupation is entitled to, reconcile it against what the ATO already holds, and lodge it once you’ve approved it. Because we lodge as your agent, you usually get until the following May instead of 31 October.

How it runs

  1. 01 Verify your identity online — about three minutes on your phone, included in the fee. Every client, every service, no exceptions. How it works.
  2. 02 Tell us about your year online: work, investments, anything you want to claim.
  3. 03 We pull your income statement, interest, dividends and health cover from the ATO.
  4. 04 An accountant prepares the return and asks about anything that doesn’t add up.
  5. 05 You see the result and approve it. We lodge, and most refunds arrive within two weeks of assessment.

What we check that software doesn’t

Pre-fill is good at income and bad at deductions. It doesn’t know you drove to a second job site, bought protective gear, paid for a union membership or worked from home three days a week. We ask about the claims that apply to your occupation, work out which home office and car methods leave you better off, and make sure each claim is one you can back up.

We also catch what pre-fill gets wrong: a late income statement, a capital gain on shares sold years after they were bought, or a HECS-HELP repayment that changes the result. If you haven’t lodged for a few years, we bring every year up to date together. The ATO treats a voluntary catch-up far better than being chased.

What to have ready

Very little to start. Your income statement, interest and dividends come straight from the ATO. Have receipts or records for anything you want to claim, details of any property or shares you sold, and your bank account for the refund. Don’t send your tax file number through the form. We collect it securely once an accountant is on your file.
