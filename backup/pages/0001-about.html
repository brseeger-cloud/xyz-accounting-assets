About the practice

A tax agent that behaves like a software product

Most accounting practices bought a website. Most tax apps cannot actually lodge anything. We wanted the interaction quality of the second with the registration and judgement of the first.

Why the calculators are free

Because gating them would be self-defeating. A calculator behind an email form is a calculator nobody uses — the competing tools are ungated, and people leave. So we put the gate where it belongs: at lodgement, which is the part that actually needs a registered agent.

There is a second reason. The calculators are the clearest demonstration we can give of how we work. Expand any line in the Tally and it shows you the arithmetic. Nothing is hidden, nothing is rounded away, and the rates are cited back to the ATO page they came from with the date they were checked. If that is how we handle a free tool, you can reasonably infer how we handle a return.

How we work

Every return is prepared and reviewed by a registered agent before it is lodged. You see the completed return and approve it — nothing goes to the ATO without your sign-off. Fees are fixed and quoted before the work starts.

If your return genuinely does not need us, we will tell you. Sending someone to myTax costs us $99 and buys the kind of trust that is worth considerably more.
