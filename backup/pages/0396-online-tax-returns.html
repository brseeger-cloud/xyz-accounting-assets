How an online return works with us

You start online and tell us what kind of return you need. We pull the data the ATO already holds, including income statements, bank interest, dividends and private health cover, so you’re not typing in what the government already knows. A registered agent works through what’s left with you: deductions, business income, rental schedules, capital gains, and the parts pre-fill gets wrong.

You see the finished return and the result before anything is lodged. Because we lodge as your agent, most individuals get until the following May rather than 31 October, provided you’re on our client list before 31 October and your earlier returns are up to date. Our fee can be taken out of your refund, and it’s deductible next year.

Every return is prepared by BRS Advisory Pty Ltd, registered tax agent 26233684 and a CPA Australia Practice. You can check our registration on the Tax Practitioners Board register.
