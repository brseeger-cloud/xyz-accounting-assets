Tax tip

How to apply for a tax file number (TFN)

Applying for a TFN is free, and you apply directly with the ATO. Australian citizens aged 15 or over with a Strong myID can apply fully online. Most other Australian residents complete an online form and then attend a participating Post Office. Visa holders in Australia with work rights apply online using their passport.

Updated 16 September 2026 Checked against ATO guidance

What a TFN is, and why you need one

A tax file number is your personal reference with the Australian Taxation Office. You keep the same one for life, including if you change your name, move interstate or leave the country and come back.

You don’t legally have to have a TFN, but going without one costs money. If you start a job and don’t give your employer a TFN, they must withhold tax at the top rate of 47%. Banks do the same with interest on your savings. You also need a TFN to lodge a tax return, claim most Centrelink payments, and make sure your super contributions are taxed correctly.

Which way to apply

The route depends on your residency and the identity documents you have. Find the row that fits you, then follow that method only. Applying twice slows things down.

Your situation How to apply How long it takes
Australian citizen, 15 or older, with an Australian passport Fully online through ATO online services using a Strong myID Usually straight away
Australian resident without a Strong myID, or under 15 Online form on the ATO website, then an interview at a participating Australia Post outlet Up to 28 days after the interview
Australian resident who can’t get to a Post Office Paper application posted to the ATO with certified copies of your ID Up to 28 days, plus postage
Permanent migrant or temporary visitor in Australia with work rights Online using your foreign passport and visa details Up to 28 days
Living outside Australia with Australian income Paper application for individuals living outside Australia Longer. Allow for overseas post

Applying online with myID

This is the fastest route. To use it you need to be an Australian citizen aged at least 15 and able to reach a Strong identity strength in the myID app. You will need:

  • The myID app on your phone, set up with your personal email address (it must match your myGov email if you already have a myGov account)
  • A current Australian passport
  • One other Australian identity document, such as a driver licence or Medicare card
  • A one-off face check, where the app compares a selfie with your passport photo

Once your myID is Strong, you complete the TFN application in ATO online services and your number is generally issued on the spot.

Applying through Australia Post

If you can’t reach a Strong myID, most Australian residents use this route:

  1. Complete the TFN application form on the ATO website.
  2. Print the application summary, which includes your reference number.
  3. Take the summary and your original identity documents to a participating Australia Post outlet within 30 days.
  4. The ATO posts your TFN to the address on your application, usually within 28 days of the interview.

Lodging an application at the Post Office is free. Check that the outlet participates in the TFN service before you go, because not all of them do.

If you’re on a visa

Permanent migrants, and temporary visitors whose visa allows them to work, apply online using their foreign passport and visa details. You must be in Australia when you apply, and the ATO checks your visa with the Department of Home Affairs. If you’re a student or working holiday maker, apply once you’ve arrived rather than before you travel.

Other routes

There is a separate paper form for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people who can’t provide enough identity documents for the standard options, which uses a referee instead. People applying for Centrelink payments can also apply for a TFN as part of their claim with Services Australia.

If you already have a TFN but can’t find it

Don’t apply again. Your TFN appears on past notices of assessment, on your income statement in myGov, and on letters from the ATO. You can also call the ATO on 13 28 61 and they will send it to your address on file after confirming your identity.

Keeping your TFN safe

Treat your TFN like a bank PIN. Give it to your employer, bank, super fund, Centrelink and your registered tax agent, and no one else. Don’t email it, and don’t put it on a job application before you have the job.

Nobody legitimate charges a fee to get you a TFN. If a website asks for payment to apply, you’re on the wrong site. If you think your TFN has been misused, contact the ATO straight away.

Once you have it

Give your TFN to your employer on a TFN declaration so they withhold at the right rate. If you’ve already been taxed at the top rate because your TFN wasn’t provided, you’ll generally get the excess back when you lodge your tax return. The refund calculator shows roughly how much.
