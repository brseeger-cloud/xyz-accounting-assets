Business name registration

Register a business name with ASIC so you can trade under something other than your own name or your company name — after we have checked the name is available, clear of obvious trade mark conflicts, and actually needed.

Our fee

$79 fixed, inc. GST

Fixed, agreed in writing, no work begins before you accept it.

Government charges

ASIC registration fee

For a one- or three-year registration, passed on at cost and shown separately. We never mark it up.

Turnaround

1 business day

Once your ABN is active. ASIC usually registers the name the same day it is lodged.

What’s included

  • A check of whether you need a business name at all — trading under your own name, or your company’s exact name, does not
  • An availability search against the ASIC register
  • A first-pass search of the trade mark register, so you don’t build a brand on someone else’s mark
  • Registration with ASIC for one or three years, linked to your ABN
  • A reminder before the renewal falls due

How it runs

  1. 01 Verify your identity online — about three minutes on your phone, included in the fee. Every client, every service, no exceptions. How it works.
  2. 02 Tell us the name you want and the ABN it will sit under.
  3. 03 We check availability and flag anything too close to an existing name or trade mark.
  4. 04 We lodge the registration with ASIC and pay the fee on your behalf.
  5. 05 You get the ASIC confirmation, and we diary the renewal.

A business name is not a trade mark

Registering a business name lets you trade under it. It does not give you ownership of the name or stop anyone else using something similar — only a registered trade mark does that. If the name matters to the brand, we will tell you when it is worth protecting.
