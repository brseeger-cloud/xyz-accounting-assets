What a partnership is and isn’t

A partnership lodges a return so the ATO can see how the profit was split, but it does not pay tax on it. Each partner picks up their share and is taxed at their own marginal rate, which is often the whole appeal — two people on lower rates beat one person on a higher one.

The trade-off is liability. In a general partnership each partner can be pursued for the whole of the partnership’s debts, not just their share, and that includes debts the other partner ran up. It is the usual reason people move to a company or a trust later, and it is considerably cheaper to think about now.
