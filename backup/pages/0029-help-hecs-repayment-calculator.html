What changed on 1 July 2025

The old system applied a single percentage to your whole repayment income. Earning one dollar over a threshold moved you to a higher rate on every dollar you earned, so a $1 pay rise could cost several hundred dollars. It was the clearest example of a genuine tax cliff in the Australian system.

The marginal system removed it. Nothing below $69,528 counts. Above that, 15c in each dollar up to the middle band, then 17c above it. Because only the excess counts, a pay rise can never leave you worse off.

Why your repayment is higher than you expected

Repayment income is not your salary. The ATO adds back reportable fringe benefits, any super you salary sacrificed, exempt foreign employment income and net investment losses. A negatively geared property and a salary sacrifice arrangement can both cut your tax while leaving your HECS repayment untouched — or raising it.

Thresholds move every year

They are indexed to average weekly earnings. The minimum threshold went from $67,000 in 2025–26 to $69,528 in 2026–27. Switch the year selector above to see what applies to the year you are lodging, not the year you are in.

Questions people actually ask

What is the HECS repayment threshold for 2026-27?
You start repaying once your repayment income passes $69,528. Below that there is no compulsory repayment, whatever your loan balance.

How is my HECS repayment calculated now?
Marginally, since 1 July 2025. You pay 15c on each dollar above the threshold, then 17c on income above the middle band. The old system charged a single rate on your entire income, so crossing a threshold by one dollar could cost hundreds — that cliff is gone.

Is repayment income the same as my salary?
No, and this catches people out. Repayment income is your taxable income plus reportable fringe benefits, salary-sacrificed super, exempt foreign income and any net investment loss. Salary sacrificing into super lowers your tax but not your HECS repayment.

Should I pay my HECS off early?
Rarely worth it purely financially — HELP is indexed to inflation rather than charged interest, so it is among the cheapest debt available. It does affect borrowing capacity for a mortgage, which is the usual reason people clear it early.

When is my loan indexed?
On 1 June each year, applied to the balance outstanding at that point. Compulsory repayments made through your tax return are credited when the return is assessed, which can be after indexation.
